Sales Execution Guidelines
Billing shall be strictly governed by company credit policy and customer-wise credit limits. Customers blocked due to payment, credit limit, pricing, or dispatch dependency must be cleared before further billing commitment. All 75+ ageing risk accounts require strict follow-up with payment commitment date and expected amount. New leads should be converted before the 10th of the month to enable repeat billing by month-end. Minimum 80% achievement is expected at total plan level and customer / lead level. Achievement will be reviewed with the Managing Director every Friday. Risks, delays, customer objections, and escalation matters must be reported in advance; no month-end surprises are acceptable. The salesperson confirms ownership of this plan and commits to achieving the planned billing and collection actions.
